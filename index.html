Privacy Policy

Snapick · Effective 24 July 2026 · Last updated 7 August 2026

In short: Snapick does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data. All photo analysis happens on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how Snapick (the “App”), provided by Curry Fish Balls Limited (“we”, “us”), handles your information. It applies to the Snapick iOS application distributed on the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no user accounts, no login, and no server component. We do not receive, store, or transmit your photos, videos, metadata, contact details, location, device identifiers, or usage statistics.

2. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library so that it can analyze the photos you choose. This access is used solely to:

  • read the photos you select, in order to score and group them;
  • extract a still frame from a Live Photo when you ask the App to do so;
  • save enhanced copies back to your library when you choose to save.

Your original photos are never modified or overwritten. Saving always creates a new photo alongside the original, and the App may create an album named “Snapick Picks” to keep your results together.

The App includes two deletion features that only ever act on items you have individually selected and confirmed: removing similar shots you have ticked, and deleting a single screenshot via long-press. Deleted items go to your system “Recently Deleted” album. The App never deletes anything in bulk and never deletes without your explicit confirmation.

You may grant Full Access or Limited Access. Under Limited Access the App can only see the photos you have specifically allowed. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

3. On-Device Processing

All scoring, similarity detection, frame selection, and cropping are performed locally on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. No photo or derived data is ever uploaded, and the App makes no network requests other than opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use in your browser when you tap those links.

4. Purchases

Snapick Pro is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. To determine whether you have an active subscription or lifetime unlock, the App reads your entitlement record from Apple’s StoreKit framework on your device. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage

The App saves a small number of preferences on your device — for example your selected analysis quality and a cached copy of your Pro status. This data stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is removed when you delete the App.

6. Tracking and Third Parties

The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. It contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party software development kits of any kind. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any.

7. Children’s Privacy

Snapick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the App collects no data at all, no personal information from children is gathered.

8. Your Rights

The App holds no account and stores no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, export, correct, or erase on your behalf. You retain full control of everything on your device: you may delete any photo the App created, remove the “Snapick Picks” album, revoke photo permission, or delete the App entirely at any time. If you have a privacy question, or wish to exercise a right under the GDPR, CCPA, or a comparable law, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to currydev2026@outlook.com.

一、我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何个人数据。本应用没有账号体系、没有登录、没有服务器。我们不会接收、存储或传输你的照片、视频、元数据、联系方式、位置、设备标识符或使用数据。

二、相册权限

本应用申请相册权限，是为了分析你所选择的照片。该权限仅用于：

  • 读取你选中的照片，用于评分与分组；
  • 在你要求时，从实况照片中提取某一帧静态画面；
  • 在你点击保存时，把优化后的副本写回相册。

你的原始照片不会被修改或覆盖。保存始终是在原图之外新建一张照片；本应用可能会创建一个名为「Snapick 精选」的相册来集中存放结果。

本应用包含两处删除功能，且仅作用于你逐项勾选并再次确认的项目：清理你勾选的相似照片，以及长按删除单张截图。被删除的项目会进入系统的「最近删除」相册。本应用不会批量删除，也不会在未经你明确确认的情况下删除任何内容。

你可以授予"完全访问"或"有限访问"。在有限访问下，本应用只能看到你明确允许的照片。你可以随时在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中更改或撤销该权限。

三、端上处理

所有评分、相似度判定、最佳帧选择和裁剪，都使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架在你的设备本地完成。不会上传任何照片或衍生数据；除了你点击链接时用浏览器打开本隐私政策或使用条款之外，本应用不发起任何网络请求。

五、本地存储

本应用会在你的设备上保存少量偏好设置——例如所选的分析质量档位，以及 Pro 状态的本地快取。这些数据仅存于你的设备，我们无法读取，卸载应用时即被清除。
