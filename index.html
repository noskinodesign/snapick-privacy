Privacy Policy

Snapick · Effective 24 July 2026 · Last updated 9 November 2026

At a glance: Snapick analyzes your photos on your device and does not upload your photos to our servers or to TelemetryDeck. We use TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage statistics to understand product interactions and improve Snapick. These statistics are stored in the European Union, are not linked to your identity, and are not used for advertising tracking. No Snapick account is required.

This Privacy Policy explains how Snapick (the “App”), provided by Hunan Xingling Network Information Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the Snapick iOS application distributed on the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

Snapick does not require an account or a login. We use TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage statistics about interactions with the App, such as the use of its features. We use these statistics to understand how Snapick is used and improve its product experience. They do not include your photos or videos, do not identify you personally, and are not linked to your identity or used for advertising tracking.

TelemetryDeck’s SDK may include technical context with usage events, such as the app version, operating system version, device type, and an anonymized identifier used for analytics. This technical context is separate from your photo content. The analytics data is transmitted to TelemetryDeck and stored in the European Union.

If you contact us for support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and help resolve the issue. Please avoid sending photos or other personal information that is not needed for support.

2. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library so that it can analyze the photos you choose. This access is used solely to:

  • read the photos you select, in order to score and group them;
  • extract a still frame from a Live Photo when you ask the App to do so;
  • save enhanced copies back to your library when you choose to save.

Your original photos are never modified or overwritten. Saving always creates a new photo alongside the original, and the App may create an album named “Snapick Picks” to keep your results together.

The App includes two deletion features that only ever act on items you have individually selected and confirmed: removing similar shots you have ticked, and deleting a single screenshot via long-press. Deleted items go to your system “Recently Deleted” album. The App never deletes anything in bulk and never deletes without your explicit confirmation.

You may grant Full Access or Limited Access. Under Limited Access the App can only see the photos you have specifically allowed. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

3. On-Device Processing

Photo scoring, similarity analysis, frame selection, and cropping are performed locally on your device. Snapick does not upload your photos, videos, or the image content used for this analysis to our servers or to TelemetryDeck.

Local photo processing does not mean that the App never uses the internet. Snapick sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck as described in this policy. Purchases and purchase restoration may communicate with Apple through StoreKit, and opening external links also uses the internet. These activities do not upload your photo library to us or to TelemetryDeck.

4. Purchases

Snapick Pro is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Payment is processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. To determine whether you have an active subscription or lifetime unlock, the App reads your entitlement record from Apple’s StoreKit framework on your device. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage

Snapick stores preferences and cached information on your device, such as your selected analysis quality and a cached copy of your Pro entitlement status. These local records support the App’s operation. Anonymous usage statistics are handled separately as described in Sections 1 and 6.

Deleting the App does not itself request deletion of analytics data previously transmitted to TelemetryDeck or support correspondence previously sent to us. Photos saved to your photo library remain under your control in the Photos app.

6. Tracking and Third Parties

Snapick does not contain advertising and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising or advertising measurement. We do not sell your data.

The App integrates the TelemetryDeck SDK, provided by TelemetryDeck GmbH, for anonymous product interaction analytics. We transmit anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck for this purpose. These statistics do not include your photos or videos, are not linked to your identity, and are stored in the European Union. See TelemetryDeck’s Privacy FAQ for information about its analytics service.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit (see Section 4).

7. Children’s Privacy

Snapick is not directed at children under 13. The anonymous usage analytics described in this policy do not identify users personally. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, including through a support request, please contact vibesay@163.com so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

You can change or revoke photo library access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. You can delete photos created by Snapick, remove the “Snapick Picks” album, or delete the App. Snapick does not require an account, so there is no Snapick account to delete.

For privacy questions or requests to exercise rights under the GDPR, CCPA, or other applicable data protection laws, contact vibesay@163.com. We will assess and respond to requests as required by applicable law. Because our analytics data is anonymous and not linked to your identity, we may be unable to identify which analytics records relate to you. This does not prevent you from contacting us about information you have provided directly, such as support correspondence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our data practices change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by applicable law.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to vibesay@163.com.

隐私政策

Snapick · 生效日期 2026 年 7 月 24 日 · 最后更新 2026 年 11 月 9 日

简要说明：Snapick 在你的设备本地分析照片，不会将照片上传至我们或 TelemetryDeck 的服务器。我们使用 TelemetryDeck 收集匿名使用统计，以了解产品交互并改善 Snapick。统计数据存储在欧盟，不关联你的个人身份，也不用于广告跟踪。使用 Snapick 无需注册 Snapick 账号。

本隐私政策说明由 湖南星灵网络信息有限公司（"我们"）提供的 Snapick（"本应用"）如何处理信息，适用于在 Apple App Store 发行的 Snapick iOS 应用。

一、我们收集的信息

Snapick 不要求注册账号或登录。我们使用 TelemetryDeck 收集有关应用交互的匿名使用统计，例如应用功能的使用情况。这些统计用于了解 Snapick 的使用方式并改善产品体验，不包含你的照片或视频，不可用于识别你的个人身份，不与你的个人身份关联，也不用于广告跟踪。

TelemetryDeck SDK 可能随使用事件附带技术信息，例如应用版本、操作系统版本、设备类型，以及用于统计分析的匿名化标识。这些技术信息与照片内容相互独立。统计数据会发送至 TelemetryDeck，并存储在欧盟。

如果你主动联系我们寻求支持，我们会收到你发送邮件时使用的邮箱地址、邮件内容，以及你选择提供的附件。我们使用这些信息回复请求并协助解决问题。请避免提供支持所不需要的照片或其他个人信息。

二、相册权限

本应用申请相册权限，是为了分析你所选择的照片。该权限仅用于：

  • 读取你选中的照片，用于评分与分组；
  • 在你要求时，从实况照片中提取某一帧静态画面；
  • 在你点击保存时，把优化后的副本写回相册。

你的原始照片不会被修改或覆盖。保存始终是在原图之外新建一张照片；本应用可能会创建一个名为「Snapick 精选」的相册来集中存放结果。

本应用包含两处删除功能，且仅作用于你逐项勾选并再次确认的项目：清理你勾选的相似照片，以及长按删除单张截图。被删除的项目会进入系统的「最近删除」相册。本应用不会批量删除，也不会在未经你明确确认的情况下删除任何内容。

你可以授予"完全访问"或"有限访问"。在有限访问下，本应用只能看到你明确允许的照片。你可以随时在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中更改或撤销该权限。

三、端上处理

照片评分、相似度分析、最佳帧选择和裁剪均在你的设备本地完成。Snapick 不会将你的照片、视频或用于这些分析的图像内容上传至我们或 TelemetryDeck 的服务器。

照片本地处理不代表本应用完全不联网。Snapick 会按照本政策向 TelemetryDeck 发送匿名使用统计。购买及恢复购买可能通过 StoreKit 与 Apple 通信，打开外部链接也会使用网络。这些活动不会将你的照片图库上传给我们或 TelemetryDeck。

四、内购

Snapick Pro 通过 Apple 的 App 内购买系统销售。支付全部由 Apple 处理，我们不会看到也不会存储你的支付信息。为判断你是否拥有有效订阅或终身解锁，本应用读取设备上 Apple StoreKit 框架中的权益记录。Apple 对购买数据的处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

五、本地存储

Snapick 会在你的设备上保存偏好设置和缓存信息，例如所选的分析质量档位，以及 Pro 权益状态的本地缓存。这些本地记录用于支持应用运行。匿名使用统计按照本政策第一节和第六节另行处理。

删除本应用本身不会向 TelemetryDeck 发出删除先前已传输统计数据的请求，也不会删除你之前发送给我们的支持邮件。已经保存至系统相册的照片，仍由你通过「照片」应用管理。

六、跟踪与第三方

Snapick 不包含广告，也不会为了广告投放或广告效果衡量而跨其他公司的应用和网站跟踪你。我们不出售你的数据。

本应用集成由 TelemetryDeck GmbH 提供的 TelemetryDeck SDK，用于匿名产品交互分析。为此，我们会向 TelemetryDeck 传输匿名使用统计。这些统计不包含你的照片或视频，不关联你的个人身份，并存储在欧盟。有关该统计服务的说明，请参阅TelemetryDeck 隐私常见问题。

App 内购买通过 StoreKit 由 Apple 处理（见第四节）。

七、儿童隐私

Snapick 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。本政策所述的匿名使用统计不识别用户的个人身份。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童向我们提供了个人信息，包括通过支持请求提供信息，请联系 vibesay@163.com，以便我们核查并采取适当措施。

八、你的权利

你可以在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中更改或撤销相册访问权限，也可以删除 Snapick 生成的照片、移除「Snapick 精选」相册，或删除本应用。Snapick 不要求注册账号，因此不存在需要注销的 Snapick 账号。

如有隐私疑问，或希望行使 GDPR、CCPA 或其他适用数据保护法律赋予的权利，请联系 vibesay@163.com。我们会按照适用法律评估并处理请求。由于统计数据是匿名的，且不关联你的个人身份，我们可能无法确定哪些统计记录与你有关。这不影响你就自己直接提供的信息（例如支持邮件）联系我们。

九、政策变更

当数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。修订后的政策会公布在本页面，并更新"最后更新"日期。对于重大变更，我们会在适用法律要求时另行通知或征求同意。

十、联系方式

与本隐私政策相关的问题，请发送至 vibesay@163.com。
