What Snapick does

Snapick looks through a batch of photos you select and helps you find the ones worth keeping. It scores each photo for sharpness, expression, stability, and composition, groups near-identical shots together, and can pull a sharper frame out of a Live Photo. Everything runs on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Does Snapick change or delete my original photos?

No. Saving always creates a new photo alongside the original, and results are collected in an album called “Snapick Picks”. Snapick never deletes anything in bulk. Two features can remove individual items — clearing similar shots you have ticked one by one, and long-pressing a single screenshot — and both ask you to confirm first. Removed items go to your system “Recently Deleted” album.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis happens locally using Apple’s Vision framework. Snapick has no server and makes no network requests other than opening the links on this site.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Snapick, tap the Pro crown button at the top right, then tap Restore Purchases. It is also available in Settings. Sign in with the same Apple Account you originally purchased with.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open Manage Subscriptions, or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Snapick Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

What is the difference between the three plans?

None in terms of features — Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime all unlock exactly the same capabilities. They differ only in billing period.

Snapick says there are no photos or no Live Photos

Snapick works on your own photo library, so it needs photos on the device. For the Live Enhance tab you need at least one Live Photo: open the Camera app, turn on the Live Photos toggle (the concentric-circles icon), take a few shots, and try again.

I chose Limited Access and Snapick can’t see my photos

Under Limited Access, Snapick only sees the photos you have specifically allowed. Tap “Choose Photos” again to add more, or grant full access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Snapick.

Snapick 是做什么的

Snapick 会分析你选中的一批照片，帮你挑出真正值得留下的那几张。它对清晰度、表情、稳定性和构图分别评分，把高度相似的照片归到一组，还能从实况照片里取出更清晰的一帧。全部处理都在你的设备上完成。

常见问题

Snapick 会修改或删除我的原始照片吗？

不会。保存始终是在原图之外新建一张照片，结果集中放在「Snapick 精选」相册里。Snapick 不会批量删除任何内容。有两处功能可以删除单个项目——清理你逐张勾选的相似照片，以及长按删除单张截图——两处都会先要求你确认。被删除的项目会进入系统的「最近删除」相册。

我的照片会被上传吗？

不会。所有分析都使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架在本地完成。Snapick 没有服务器，除了打开本站的链接之外不发起任何网络请求。

换了设备，怎么恢复购买？

打开 Snapick，点右上角的 Pro 皇冠按钮，然后点恢复购买；设置页里也有同样的入口。请使用当初购买时的同一个 Apple 账户登录。

怎么管理或取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理，不由我们处理。打开 管理订阅， 或在设备上进入「设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅」。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，以免自动续订。Snapick Pro 终身版是一次性购买，不会续订。

三个方案有什么区别？

功能上没有区别——月订阅、年订阅、终身版解锁的能力完全相同，只是计费周期不同。

提示「没有照片」或「没有实况照片」怎么办？

Snapick 分析的是你自己的相册，所以设备上需要有照片。「实况优化」还需要至少一张实况照片：打开相机 App，开启「实况」开关（同心圆图标），拍几张之后再回来。

选了「有限访问」，Snapick 看不到我的照片

有限访问下，Snapick 只能看到你明确允许的照片。再次点击「选择照片」可以添加更多，或在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → Snapick」中改为允许访问全部照片。
